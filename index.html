Strategic Operations

A Legacy of Excellence in Indian Cinema

Distribution Dominance

Based out of Hyderabad, ASTRA Motion Pictures has proved to be a dominant force in theatrical distribution. The company has successfully distributed over 150 movies across the primary theatrical circuits of Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

Strategic Autonomy

Built upon high-tier industry foundations, the studio initiated its journey under the prominent banner of SVC in close collaboration with V Venkata Ramana Reddy (Dil Raju Garu). This operational foundation paved the way for the studio's evolution into producing high-value movies entirely independently, scaling smoothly from regional roots to national operations.

The Visionary

Mr. Bikshamaiah Sangam

Founder & Chairman IMDb

Headed by Mr. Bikshamaiah Sangam, ASTRA Motion Pictures has established a significant history in the Indian entertainment industry, specializing in large-scale movie production, distribution, and financing. Driven by strategic growth, Mr. Sangam successfully expanded the studio's footprint beyond the regional Telugu film industry directly into Bollywood, marking a major national entry with the release of the acclaimed action thriller EKTA.

His production philosophy centres on discovering bold narratives, championing emerging directors, and creating films that blend commercial appeal with artistic integrity. Under his stewardship, the studio has produced critically acclaimed films across genres, establishing a legacy that spans generations of filmgoers.
