The Visionary

Bikshamaiah Sangam

Founder & Producer IMDb

Bikshamaiah Sangam is the visionary founder of ASTRA Motion Pictures Private Limited (formerly known as BS Production House), a distinguished film production house based in Hyderabad, Telangana. With an unwavering passion for storytelling, he has dedicated over two decades to nurturing cinematic talent and producing films that resonate deeply with audiences.

His independent production journey includes notable films such as the successful Nandini Nursing Home (2016), the gripping thriller Ekta (2019), the intense drama D3: Dosti Dil Dhokha (2021), and the recent compelling releases TTDS (2024) and Dil Dosti Duniyadari (2024).

His production philosophy centres on discovering bold narratives, championing emerging directors, and creating films that blend commercial appeal with artistic integrity. Under his stewardship, the studio has produced critically acclaimed films across genres establishing a legacy that spans generations of filmgoers.
